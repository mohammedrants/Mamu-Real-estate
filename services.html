From a 5 marla plot to a commercial building, we find the right property, check every paper and stay with you until the transfer is done.

Residential plots

Plots in established and upcoming societies, for families who want to build or investors who want to hold. We shortlist plots that match your budget and block preference, and we check every one before you see it.

What we check

  • Possession and allotment status
  • Development: roads, electricity, gas
  • Outstanding society dues and NOC
  • Whether the file is clear to transfer

Commercial plots & buildings

Shops, offices, plazas and commercial plots on main roads and boulevards — for running a business or for steady rental income.

What we check

  • Commercial approval and permitted use
  • Frontage, road width and visibility
  • Realistic rent for the location
  • Dues, NOCs and ownership chain

Houses & villas

Family homes, villas and mansions. We arrange viewings at times that suit you, tell you plainly what's good and what isn't, and negotiate the price on your behalf.

What we check

  • Construction quality walk-through
  • Ownership papers: fard, registry, intiqal
  • Utility bills, meters and connections
  • Price compared with recent deals nearby

Property investment

Instalment plans, pre-launch projects and resale deals. We help you weigh the return against the risk, and stay with you until possession.

What we check

  • Developer's track record and approvals
  • Instalment plan terms, in writing
  • Resale and rental outlook for the area
  • Exit options if your plans change
